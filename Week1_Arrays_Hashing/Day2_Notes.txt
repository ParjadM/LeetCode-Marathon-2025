DAY 2: TWO POINTERS & SLIDING WINDOW
Date: Nov 20, 2025

1. THE TWO POINTERS PATTERN
   - When to use: Searching for pairs in a sorted array, or checking palindromes.
   - Strategy: 
     * Start pointers at edges (L=0, R=end).
     * Move inwards based on logic (Sum too big? Decrement R. Sum too small? Increment L).
   - Complexity: Reduces O(n^2) to O(n).

2. SLIDING WINDOW PATTERN
   - When to use: Substring/Subarray problems (Longest..., Smallest..., Sum of k...).
   - Formula (The "Caterpillar"):
     1. Expand 'r' (Add to window).
     2. Check Validity (Loop `while invalid`).
     3. Shrink 'l' (Remove from window to make it valid again).
     4. Update Result (usually max(res, r-l+1)).

3. PROBLEM SPECIFIC TRICKS
   - "Valid Palindrome": Use .isalnum() to skip non-letters. Compare s[i].lower() == s[j].lower().
   - "3Sum": 
     * SORT the array first! 
     * Loop `k` through array. Then solve Two Sum on the rest.
     * CRITICAL: Skip duplicates to avoid same answers (while nums[l] == nums[l-1]).
   - "Container With Most Water": 
     * Greedy approach. Start wide. 
     * Always move the pointer of the SHORTER wall (hoping for a taller one).
   - "Longest Repeating Char Replacement":
     * Validity Check: (WindowLength - MaxFreq) <= k.
     * If > k, shrink window.

4. THE "HARD" ONE: TRAPPING RAIN WATER
   - Logic: Water at index `i` is determined by the bottleneck of the tallest walls to its left and right.
   - Formula: min(max_left[i], max_right[i]) - height[i].
   - Optimization: Pre-compute max_left and max_right arrays so you don't re-scan.

5. PYTHON SYNTAX
   - Infinity: float('inf')
   - Char check: char.isalnum()
   - Remove from set: myset.remove(val)
